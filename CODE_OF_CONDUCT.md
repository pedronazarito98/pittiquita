# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want `pittiquita` to be a respectful, useful, and welcoming open-source project. Everyone participating in the repository is expected to help create an environment free from harassment, regardless of experience, identity, background, ability, appearance, nationality, religion, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still addressing concrete impact.
- Give context, evidence, and reproducible examples when raising a problem.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and coordinated security disclosure.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and move disagreement to the appropriate forum.
- Make space for people with different levels of English, Portuguese, open-source experience, and technical context.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private information, credentials, customer data, or private correspondence without permission.
- Pressuring someone to disclose personal or security-sensitive information.
- Impersonation, misleading claims of affiliation, or presenting illustrative artifacts as real evidence.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, demo artifacts, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in a public space.

## Reporting

Use the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/pedronazarito98) to arrange a private reporting channel. Do not include conduct-report details in a public issue.

Include only the context needed to understand the concern. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, though information may need to be shared with people directly involved in resolving the situation.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate process in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, code, issues, or other contributions that violate this code. Depending on severity and pattern, consequences may include:

1. a private clarification or correction request;
2. a formal warning;
3. temporary limits on participation;
4. permanent removal from project spaces;
5. escalation to the hosting platform or relevant authorities when required for safety or law.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid conflicts of interest where possible. Good-faith reports and appeals will not be punished.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by common open-source community standards, including the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), while using project-specific language and reporting details.
